Dawn on the Amazon operates custom riverboat tours out of Iquitos, ranging from half-day excursions to multi-day expeditions into Pacaya-Samiria National Reserve. Their fleet includes fast boats and slower skiffs suited to quieter tributaries; itineraries are built around the guests rather than a fixed programme. The guides are bilingual and have lived and worked on the river for most of their lives — the kind of on-the-water knowledge that comes through in the difference between a cursory wildlife stop and a two-hour sit watching river otters on an oxbow lake. Main destinations include the Yanayacu Lakes system, the Manatee Rescue Centre outside Nauta, and various communities and wildlife corridors within the reserve. A flexible, locally owned operation suited to independent travellers.
