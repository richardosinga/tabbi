Maniti Expeditions is a local Iquitos-based agency operating the Maniti Eco-Lodge on the Maniti River, a black-water tributary of the Amazon. Black-water rivers have their own distinct ecology — lower fish diversity but better visibility for spotting aquatic wildlife, and a strong association with pink river dolphins, which are common in the Maniti's calmer reaches. The typical programme includes piranha fishing in the afternoon, night boat trips for caiman spotting along the riverbanks, medicinal plant walks with indigenous guides, and morning canoe trips through flooded forest in season. A genuinely local operation with close community ties and guides who know this stretch of river well.
