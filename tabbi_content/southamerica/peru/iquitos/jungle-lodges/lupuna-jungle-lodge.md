Lupuna sits four hours by boat from Iquitos on a tributary of the Napo River, which puts it firmly inside primary forest territory. The operation is deliberately small — a maximum of twelve guests — and family-run, which keeps the experience personal and the guiding consistent. Bilingual guides specialise in birding and wildlife spotting; the primary forest surrounding the lodge is relatively undisturbed, and species lists here run longer than at lodges closer to the city. Accommodation is simple but comfortable, and the remoteness is part of the appeal: this is not a place for travellers who want to be close to Iquitos. Price is given on request depending on group size, duration and season.
