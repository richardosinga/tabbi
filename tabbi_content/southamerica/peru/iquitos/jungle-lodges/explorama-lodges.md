Explorama has been running lodges from Iquitos since 1964, making it the most established operation in the Peruvian Amazon by some distance. Their network of camps spreads along the Napo and Sucusari rivers at distances of 80 km and 160 km from Iquitos, which means the forest quality and wildlife encounter rates improve significantly with each step further out. The furthest camp, ExplorNapo, sits adjacent to a 500-metre treetop canopy walkway suspended 35 metres above the forest floor — one of the longest in South America. Expert naturalist guides lead all activities, from dawn bird walks to night canoeing for caimans. A reliable choice for travellers who want genuine primary forest with experienced guiding and reasonable comfort.
