Iquitos is the gateway to the largest untouched rainforest in Peru. Most jungle tours operate out of lodges between one and four hours by boat from the city, placing guests inside primary or secondary forest. Activities include canoe trips, night walks, piranha fishing, visits to indigenous Yagua communities and — in flooded season (November–May) — journeys through the Amazon's flooded forest. The Pacaya-Samiria National Reserve, accessible from Iquitos, is one of the most biodiverse protected areas in South America. Three-day minimum stays are recommended; shorter trips rarely get beyond the suburban margins of the forest.
