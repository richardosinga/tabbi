Amazon Planet operates a comfortable riverine eco-lodge in secondary forest bordering primary Amazon, roughly two hours by fast boat from Iquitos. Activities include morning and night canoe trips for caimans and pink river dolphins, jungle walks to identify medicinal plants, and a visit to a traditional Yagua community for demonstrations of blowgun hunting and traditional crafts. The lodge has electricity until 10pm, net-protected rooms and simple but good food. At this price point it offers a good balance between comfort and genuine wilderness access.
