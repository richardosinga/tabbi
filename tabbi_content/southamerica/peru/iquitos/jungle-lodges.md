Iquitos sits at the heart of the largest tropical rainforest on earth, and the lodges fanning out from the city along the Amazon, Napo and Maniti rivers are the best way to experience it properly. The further from the city, the better the forest: primary jungle begins roughly 80 km out, and the difference in wildlife encounters — particularly primates, macaws and river dolphins — is significant. Most lodges operate on all-inclusive packages covering meals, guides and activities. The Amazon floods seasonally between November and May, which changes what is accessible but also opens up flooded-forest canoe routes that are only possible during high water.
