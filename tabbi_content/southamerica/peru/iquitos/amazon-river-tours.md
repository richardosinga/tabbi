Not everyone has the time or budget for a multi-night lodge stay, and day tours from Iquitos still reach genuine jungle within a couple of hours by fast boat. The main destinations from the city are the Pacaya-Samiria National Reserve — the largest flooded forest reserve in South America — the Yanayacu oxbow lakes, and the Manatee Rescue Centre on the road to Nauta. Boat operators range from freelance guides on local lanchas to established companies with English-speaking naturalists and dedicated river vessels. On any river trip, pink river dolphin sightings are realistic; caiman, giant river otters and tropical birds are common along the quieter tributaries.
