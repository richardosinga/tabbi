Explorama has been running Amazon lodges from Iquitos since 1964 and remains the most established operation in the region. Their network of four camps sits at 40 km, 80 km, 140 km and 160 km from Iquitos respectively, meaning the forest quality improves significantly with each step. The flagship is ExplorNapo Lodge at 140 km, adjacent to a 500-metre treetop canopy walkway at 35 metres above the forest floor. Naturalist guides lead all activities. The most comfortable option for travellers who want genuine primary forest without roughing it entirely.
