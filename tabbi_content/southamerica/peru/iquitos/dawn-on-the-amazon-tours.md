Dawn on the Amazon is run by Bill Grimes, an American naturalist who has lived in Iquitos for decades and is considered one of the most knowledgeable guides on the Peruvian Amazon. Tours are genuinely custom — groups are small (often just two to four people), and the itinerary is built around the guests' interests, whether that's birding, macro photography, medicinal plants or cultural exchanges with river communities. This is not a packaged lodge tour; accommodation varies from local guesthouses to camping depending on the route. Best for serious naturalists and independent travellers.
