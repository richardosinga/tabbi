Desert Adventure claims to have started the Huacachina dune buggy format in 2001 and still operates their own fleet from an office inside Desert Nights Hostel at the oasis. The standard 2-hour sunset tour drives up and over the largest dunes with multiple sandboarding stops on the way down. Full-day tours to more remote dunes further from the oasis are also available for those who want to escape the crowds. Walk-up pricing in soles at the hostel office is typically cheaper than the USD rates advertised online.
