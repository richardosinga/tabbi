Angel Desert Tours operates from Hospedaje Claudia on the Huacachina oasis strip and is one of the few dune buggy operators to publish a clear soles price on their website rather than inflated USD rates. At S/. 65 per person the pricing is transparent and competitive for a direct booking. The standard 2-hour sunset tour covers sandboarding on the dunes surrounding the oasis. Paying in soles directly at the oasis consistently works out cheaper than booking in dollars online.
