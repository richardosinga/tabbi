Desert Ica's extreme tour uses a modified dune buggy with a roll cage and takes routes over steeper dune faces than standard operators. The 2.5-hour session continues after sunset for 30–40 minutes of dune driving by headlight, which adds a different dimension to the experience. This is the highest-energy option in Huacachina and not suitable for people with back problems, vertigo or young children. The additional cost over standard operators covers the longer duration and more specialised vehicle. Book at their office near the Huacachina lagoon.
