Huacachina Experience pitches their tour at backpackers and budget travellers who want more sandboarding and less white-knuckle buggy driving. The vehicle moves more slowly between dunes, allowing more runs per person at each stop — you'll do around five or six descents rather than the three or four typical of speed-focused operators. The tradeoff is that the experience is less adrenaline-heavy. A good choice if you have children or prefer sandboarding to dune-racing. The price is the lowest available from a legitimate local operator.
