Huacachina sits in a natural lake at the centre of dunes up to 100 metres high — among the largest sand dunes accessible from a town anywhere in the world. Dune buggy tours typically run two hours at sunset, driving a 4WD buggy up and over the steepest dunes, then returning repeatedly to sandboard down. The buggy ride itself is a fair amount of it: steep descents at speed on sand. Sandboards are waxed wood boards ridden sitting or lying down; standing is possible with practice. Evenings end with the sun dropping behind the dune ridge into the Pacific sky.
