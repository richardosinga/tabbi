Desert Adventures runs the standard Huacachina dune buggy tour that most visitors do. The vehicle holds up to eight passengers and the driver takes a circuit of the five highest dunes surrounding the oasis, stopping at each for one or two sandboard descents. The tour starts 90 minutes before sunset so you catch the golden light from the highest point. Wax is applied to boards at each stop. This is a fun, loud, slightly chaotic experience — exactly what most people are here for. Book at their office by the lagoon or through GetYourGuide.
