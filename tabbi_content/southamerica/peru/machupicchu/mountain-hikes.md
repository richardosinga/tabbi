Three hikes require permits in addition to the citadel entrance: Huayna Picchu (the sharp peak visible in all the classic photos), Machu Picchu Mountain (higher and quieter), and the Sun Gate trail to Inti Punku (accessible without an extra permit). Huayna Picchu and Machu Picchu Mountain permits sell out months in advance during peak season. The hikes are steep and the altitude is significant (the citadel sits at 2,430m; the mountain peaks are above 3,000m). Guided ascents are available and recommended for those less confident with altitude.
