Machu Picchu Mountain is taller than Huayna Picchu and less well known, which means permits are slightly easier to obtain and crowds on the trail are thinner. The ascent from the citadel entrance takes around two hours on a stone staircase through cloud forest; the summit at 3,082m offers views in every direction, including down into the Urubamba River canyon. Llama Path's guide keeps a steady altitude-conscious pace and explains the ecology and Inca use of the mountain along the way. Their permit booking service is included in the price.
