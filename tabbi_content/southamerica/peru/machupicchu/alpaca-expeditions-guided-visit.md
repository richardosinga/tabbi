Alpaca Expeditions is a locally owned agency founded by a family from Aguas Calientes, and their site guides are often from the immediate community. The two-hour tour covers the core sectors — terraces, main plaza, temples and the Sun Gate viewpoint — at a brisk but unhurried pace. English is good throughout. This is the most competitively priced option among operators with consistent quality. Alpaca also operates Inca Trail and Salkantay Trek departures and can combine the site visit with trekking packages.
