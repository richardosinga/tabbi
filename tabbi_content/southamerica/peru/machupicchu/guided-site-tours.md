Machu Picchu is visually overwhelming on arrival, and without a guide the significance of individual structures — the Temple of the Sun, the Intihuatana stone, the agricultural terraces — is easy to miss. Guided tours last two to three hours and cover the main sectors of the site, typically in small groups of up to eight. The best guides are licensed by Peru's Ministry of Culture and often have archaeology or history backgrounds. Tours can be booked in advance or occasionally picked up from operators in Aguas Calientes the evening before.
