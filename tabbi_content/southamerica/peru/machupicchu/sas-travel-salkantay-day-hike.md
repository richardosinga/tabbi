The Sun Gate hike is the only mountain route at Machu Picchu that doesn't require an additional permit beyond the standard citadel ticket. The trail follows the final section of the Inca Trail to Inti Punku, gaining around 300m in 1.5 km — a steady climb that takes 45–60 minutes. The view from the gate at sunrise, when mist fills the valley below the citadel, is one of the most photographed in Peru. SAS Travel provides a guide and small group (maximum ten); the hike can be combined with a citadel tour in the same day.
