Huayna Picchu permits are notoriously hard to secure independently and sell out months ahead. Alpaca Expeditions include permit booking within their package, which makes the process significantly easier. The guided ascent takes around 90 minutes each way on steep Inca steps; the guide sets a sustainable pace. At the summit (2,720m) the views down to the citadel are spectacular and unlike any other viewpoint. The tour departs from the citadel gate; citadel entrance tickets are purchased separately by the client.
