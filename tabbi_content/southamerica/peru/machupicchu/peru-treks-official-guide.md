Peru Treks is a respected Cuzco agency with a strong track record on the Inca Trail and at Machu Picchu itself. Their site guides are trained in archaeoastronomy and place particular emphasis on how the Inca aligned the citadel's key structures with solar events — the Intihuatana stone, the Temple of the Sun and the Room of the Three Windows. The standard guided visit runs three hours and covers all principal sectors. Book through their website at least a week in advance during peak season (June–September).
