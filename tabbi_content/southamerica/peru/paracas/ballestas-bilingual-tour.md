This English-bilingual version of the Ballestas tour is worth the small premium if you want to actually understand what you're seeing. The guide explains the mechanics of the Humboldt Current that makes this part of the Pacific so productive, the difference between the three penguin species on the islands, and the ecology of the guano trade that made this coast economically significant in the 19th century. Smaller boat than the standard operator tours (maximum 15 passengers) means a quieter experience close to the wildlife.
