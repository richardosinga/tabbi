The Paracas National Reserve protects one of South America's richest coastal desert ecosystems — red-tinged cliffs, flamingo lagoons, fossil beds and dramatic Pacific viewpoints. The iconic La Catedral rock arch partially collapsed in the 2007 earthquake but the reserve's other formations and flamingo spots remain. Tours run by 4WD vehicle or buggy and typically last 3–4 hours, covering the Candelabro viewpoint, the flamingo lagoon, fossil beds and multiple clifftop lookouts. A separate SERNANP entrance fee applies at the gate.
