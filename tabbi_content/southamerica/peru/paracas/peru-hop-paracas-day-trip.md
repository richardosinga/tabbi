Peru Hop's hop-on-hop-off bus stops in Paracas for a full day en route between Lima and Nazca/Ica, making the Ballestas tour convenient for travellers on the coastal circuit. The day includes the morning Ballestas boat trip (8am departure), a guide-led drive through the Paracas National Reserve with a stop at the La Mina beach and red-sand viewpoints, and lunch in the village. This is the most convenient option if you don't want to arrange your own transport to Paracas from Ica or Lima.
