The same family-run Los Frayles operation that runs Ballestas boat tours also offers a budget vehicle circuit of the Paracas National Reserve. The 3–4 hour tour hits the main viewpoints: El Candelabro, the La Catedral cliff viewpoint, the flamingo lagoon and fossil beds. At $13 per person it is among the lowest direct prices for a guided reserve circuit in Paracas. Combining both the Ballestas and reserve tours through Los Frayles on the same day is a practical option.
