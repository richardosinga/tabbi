Emotion Tour Peru is a registered Peruvian business (EIRL) based in Paracas, offering higher-end reserve circuits with bilingual guides and small groups. Their 4-hour tours cover the reserve's main highlights — the La Catedral viewpoint, flamingo lagoon, fossil beds and clifftop Pacific panoramas — by vehicle, with trekking and biking options also available. They also run Ballestas Islands boat tours, making combined day itineraries straightforward to arrange through a single operator.
