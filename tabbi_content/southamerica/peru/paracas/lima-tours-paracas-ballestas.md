Lima Tours' Paracas day trip is the premium option for travellers based in Lima who want to see the Ballestas Islands without navigating public buses. Private transport leaves Lima after breakfast, arriving in Paracas in time for the 10am boat tour. The afternoon is spent in the Paracas National Reserve with a bilingual guide explaining the Paracas culture (pre-dating the Inca) and the desert ecology. Return to Lima by early evening. The private vehicle and included lunch make this substantially more expensive than the local alternative but eliminates all logistics.
