The Ballestas Islands, known as the "poor man's Galapagos", shelter the largest concentration of marine wildlife on Peru's coast. A two-hour boat tour passes sea lion beaches, penguin colonies, Peruvian booby and cormorant nesting sites, and a red-footed booby rookery. On the way out, boats pass the famous Paracas Candelabra — a 180-metre geoglyph scratched into the clifftop that may predate the Nazca Lines. Tours depart from the Paracas port daily at 8am and 10am and cannot land on the islands. Binoculars are recommended.
