Los Frayles is a family-run hotel and tour operator located steps from the El Chaco pier in Paracas. They operate their own fleet including a catamaran, making them one of the few genuine boat owners rather than resellers on the dock. The Ballestas tour departs in the morning and takes roughly two hours around the islands; note that the separate SERNANP fee of S/. 16–17 is paid in cash at the dock. They also offer the Paracas National Reserve vehicle circuit.
