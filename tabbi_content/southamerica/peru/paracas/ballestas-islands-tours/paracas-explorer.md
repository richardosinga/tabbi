Paracas Explorer claims 50,000+ passengers per year and operates their own twin-engine motorboat out of El Chaco pier. Fixed departures at 08:00 and 10:00 make scheduling straightforward; the two-hour circuit covers sea lions, Humboldt penguins, Inca terns and the Candelabro geoglyph on the cliffs. The separate SERNANP fee of S/. 16–17 is paid in cash at the dock. Combined island plus Paracas National Reserve packages are also available.
