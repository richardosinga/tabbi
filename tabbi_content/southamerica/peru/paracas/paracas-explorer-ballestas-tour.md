This is the baseline Ballestas Islands tour offered by local Paracas boat operators and it does exactly what it says. A fast speedboat leaves the Paracas port at 8am or 10am and circuits the islands for two hours, with a guide (usually in Spanish only) naming the wildlife. The same tour is sold by multiple operators at the port for roughly the same price; the boats and itinerary are essentially identical. Arrive 15 minutes early to secure a window seat. Cold on the water even in summer — bring a layer.
