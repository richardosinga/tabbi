Urban Adventures operate this well-priced introductory tour daily from Parque Kennedy in Miraflores. The guide takes a small group through the Surquillo market, stopping at fishmonger stalls to explain *ceviche* before you eat it, then on to street carts for *anticuchos* and a *picaronería* for the doughnut-like fried sweet-potato rings. The tour ends with a pisco sour demonstration. Good for first-time visitors who want a readable overview of Lima's food in a half-morning.
