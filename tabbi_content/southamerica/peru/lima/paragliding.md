The cliffs of Miraflores drop straight into the Pacific, and the updrafts make them one of the best urban paragliding spots in South America. Tandem flights launch from the Parque Raimondi clifftop and stay airborne for 15–30 minutes depending on conditions. On clear days (usually June through November) you can see the whole sweep of the *Costa Verde* from Chorrillos to La Punta. Operators are clustered at the cliff edge — book in advance at weekends.
