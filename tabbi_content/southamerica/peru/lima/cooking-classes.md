Peruvian cuisine is regularly ranked among the world's best — a cooking class here is genuinely worthwhile. The best Lima classes cover ceviche technique, causa, and a pisco sour; some start with a market tour to source the ingredients. Most run in small groups in home kitchens or rooftop spaces in Miraflores or Barranco.
