Buen Provecho is a small Lima-based operator running food tours with a focus on the historic centre rather than Miraflores. The route covers the Barrio Chino (Calle Capón), stopping at *chifas* to try Peruvian-Chinese cooking, then moving through the older *picanterías* of the downtown for *causa limeña* and *ají de gallina*. Groups are kept under ten. A good choice if you're already planning to see the historic centre and want to combine sightseeing with eating.
