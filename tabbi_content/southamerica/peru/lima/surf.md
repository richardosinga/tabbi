Lima's Miraflores coastline has consistent beach breaks suitable for beginners. Playa Makaha is the main lesson spot — calm enough for first-timers, sheltered by the cliffs. Wetsuits are essential (the Pacific here is cold year-round). Most schools offer 90-minute group lessons from around $25, with equipment included.
