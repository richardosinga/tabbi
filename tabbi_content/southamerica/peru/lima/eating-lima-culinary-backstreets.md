Culinary Backstreets runs highly regarded food-focused walks in cities worldwide, and their Lima tour is among the best introductions to the city's eating culture. A maximum of eight guests walk through Miraflores and Barranco with a guide who actually writes about Peruvian food, stopping at market stalls, a *cevichería*, a *picaronería* and a bar serving craft pisco. The emphasis is on context — why Lima cooks the way it does — not just tasting.
