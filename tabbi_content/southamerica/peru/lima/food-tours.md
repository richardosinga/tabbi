Lima is one of the great food cities of the world and a guided tour is the best way to get beneath the surface — from the wholesale stalls of Mercado Surquillo to the *anticucho* carts of Barranco. Most tours include a mix of market visits, tastings and sit-down stops, covering Peruvian-Japanese *nikkei*, *ceviche*, *lomo saltado* and *picarones* in a single morning.
