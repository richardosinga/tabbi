Lima's historic centre is a UNESCO World Heritage site with colonial architecture, catacombs and a central market that few tourists reach on their own. Barranco is the city's bohemian neighbourhood — street art, clifftop bars and the Bridge of Sighs. Several independent operators run daily tours of both; the free tip-based options are genuinely good.
