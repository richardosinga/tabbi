Lima Walking Tour operates from two Tourist Information Centres in Miraflores. Their programme goes beyond the standard route — specialised tours cover the Central Market and Chinatown foodie trail, the San Francisco catacombs, and a Magic Water Circuit evening tour. All walking tours are tip-based; the specialised ones have a small fee. Site content updated July 2025. Email: info@limawalkingtour.com.
