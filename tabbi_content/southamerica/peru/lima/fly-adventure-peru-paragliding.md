Fly Adventure Peru has been operating at the Parque Raimondi clifftop since the early 2000s and is the best-known name among Miraflores paragliding outfits. Pilots are all certified and the equipment is maintained to international standards. The standard package includes a tandem flight of up to 30 minutes and a GoPro video of the flight. Weight limit is 100 kg; minimum age is 12. Flights run daily, weather permitting — the operator will advise on the morning of your booking.
