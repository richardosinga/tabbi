Andean Trail offers the most competitively priced paragliding on the Miraflores cliff, making them a good choice for travellers who want the experience without the premium. The 15–20 minute tandem flight covers the same Pacific views as other operators. Photos and video can be added for an extra fee. The pilots speak English and Spanish and are experienced with first-time flyers. Advance booking is strongly recommended at weekends between June and October.
