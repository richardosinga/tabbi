Naturaleza Activa caters to travellers who want more than a viewpoint tour. Their Colca Classic includes a guided walk down into the canyon floor — a 4-hour descent — with the option to raft the Río Chili near Arequipa on the return day. Groups are capped at eight and the guides have strong outdoor credentials. The pace is more demanding than standard Colca tours; moderate fitness is required. This is the best Colca option if you're combining it with active outdoor experiences and want to physically be in the canyon rather than just looking at it.
