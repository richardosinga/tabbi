Colca Canyon, 160 km north of Arequipa, is twice the depth of the Grand Canyon. Most tours take two days, departing early from Arequipa and stopping at the high-altitude puna before descending into the canyon. The Cruz del Cóndor viewpoint, where Andean condors ride thermals above the gorge from around 9am, is the centrepiece. A second day allows time in the colonial villages of Yanque and Chivay, with their pre-Inca terracing and hot springs. One-day tours are possible but rushed.
