Chef Arthur Garcia trained at Cordon Bleu Lima and runs classes Monday to Friday starting at 10:00 with a visit to San Pedro market to select ingredients before cooking. The Andean Menu (the higher-priced option) focuses on Arequipeño dishes: *rocoto relleno* and *chupe de camarones*. The criollo option covers broader Peruvian classics. Groups are capped at six, making this one of the most intimate cooking experiences in Arequipa. The market visit adds context about highland ingredients that class-only formats miss.
