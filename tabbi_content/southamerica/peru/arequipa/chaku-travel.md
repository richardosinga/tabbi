Chaku Travel runs four daily free walking tours departing from their physical office at Calle San Francisco 204 — at 10:00, 12:30, 15:00 and 17:15 — in both English and Spanish. Tips are the only payment. The tour covers the Plaza de Armas, the main churches, and the sillar architecture of the UNESCO-listed centre. They also offer a paid Ruta del Sillar tour along the volcanic road where the building stone was quarried, which is a worthwhile add-on for anyone with a half-day to spare.
