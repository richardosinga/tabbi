Arequipeño cuisine is one of Peru's most distinctive regional traditions, quite different from Lima or Cusco. The signature dishes — *rocoto relleno* (spicy stuffed red pepper), *chupe de camarones* (prawn chowder with Andean herbs), and *solterito* (bean and cheese salad) — all depend on ingredients specific to the highlands and river valleys around the city. Cooking classes typically run 90 minutes to three hours and include a market visit; most take small groups of under twelve.
