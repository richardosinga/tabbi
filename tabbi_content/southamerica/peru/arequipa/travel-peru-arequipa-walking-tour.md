This is a solid introductory walking tour run by a local Arequipa guide company. The three-hour route starts at the Plaza de Armas, explains the volcanic *sillar* architecture, enters the grounds of Santa Catalina Monastery (entrance fee included in price), visits the cathedral and La Compañía, and ends with a walk through San Camilo market. Groups are kept under twelve. A good orientation for first-time visitors before they explore independently. Most reviews praise the market section as a highlight.
