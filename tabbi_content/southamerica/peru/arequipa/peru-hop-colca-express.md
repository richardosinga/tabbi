Peru Hop is a hop-on-hop-off bus service for travellers moving between Peruvian cities, and their Colca Canyon add-on is one of the most affordable ways to see the gorge. The two-day tour departs Arequipa early and can drop passengers directly in Puno rather than returning to Arequipa — useful if you're heading to Lake Titicaca. The guide speaks English and the vehicle is comfortable. The trade-off for the lower price is larger group sizes (up to 20) and less flexibility on timings.
