Colca Trek has been running Colca Canyon tours from Arequipa since the 1990s and is widely regarded as the best local operator. Their two-day tour departs at 3am to time the arrival at Cruz del Cóndor with peak condor activity at 9–10am, then spends the rest of the first day walking the canyon rim. Overnight accommodation is in Chivay's hot-spring-fed hostel. Day two covers the Yanque weaving cooperative and Maca village before returning to Arequipa. Groups are kept under ten. A good value for quality.
