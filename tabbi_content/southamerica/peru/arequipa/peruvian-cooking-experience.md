Classes are held inside the garden of the Casa de Avila Hotel in the Vallecito district, a colonial property with an open kitchen suited to small groups. Sessions run Monday to Saturday at 11:00 and 16:00 with a maximum of ten to eleven participants; you cook and eat *rocoto relleno* and *solterito arequipeña* with an English-speaking instructor. Booking is via WhatsApp. One of the few Arequipa cooking schools that explicitly focuses on local Arequipeño dishes rather than a general Peruvian menu.
