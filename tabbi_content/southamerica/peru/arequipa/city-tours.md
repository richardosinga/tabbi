Arequipa's historic centre is one of the finest colonial ensembles in South America, built almost entirely from white *sillar* volcanic stone. A walking tour typically covers the Plaza de Armas, the Jesuit church of La Compañía, and the vast Santa Catalina Monastery — a city within a city that was closed to the public until 1970. Most tours also visit the San Camilo market, where Arequipa's distinctive food culture — *rocoto relleno*, *chupe de camarones*, *adobo* — is on display. Half-day tours finish by early afternoon, leaving time for the Colca Canyon preparations.
