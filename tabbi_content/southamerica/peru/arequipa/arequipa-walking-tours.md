Arequipa's historic centre is built almost entirely from *sillar*, white volcanic stone quarried from Chachani volcano — hence the city's nickname La Ciudad Blanca. A walking tour is the best way to understand the architecture: the cathedral and La Compañía church on the Plaza de Armas, the vast Santa Catalina Monastery (a self-contained city founded in 1580), and the volcanic arch viewpoints of Yanahuara looking back towards El Misti. Most tours run two to three hours and depart multiple times daily.
