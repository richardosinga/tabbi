Pablo Tour is run by Edwin, a certified ecologist and government-licensed guide who deliberately avoids large tourist circuits. Tours take off-the-beaten-path routes through Colca and extend to the deeper Cotahuasi Canyon on longer itineraries. The operation is referenced in Lonely Planet for responsible tourism practices. Best suited to independent travellers who want expert ecological context and are willing to trade the convenience of a fixed group departure for a more personalised experience.
