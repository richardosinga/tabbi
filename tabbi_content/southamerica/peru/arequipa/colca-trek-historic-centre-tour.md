This longer city tour from Colca Trek goes beyond the central plaza to cover the Yanahuara neighbourhood (with its volcanic stone archway looking back to El Misti), the 16th-century Mansión del Fundador outside the city, and the Museo Santuarios Andinos where the preserved Inca ice mummy Juanita is displayed. At four hours it is better suited to travellers spending a full day in Arequipa before the Colca trip. The Juanita museum section is the most distinctive element and not covered by cheaper tours.
