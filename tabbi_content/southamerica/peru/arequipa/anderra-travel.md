Anderra Travel operates from Calle Palacio Viejo 206-B in the historic centre and offers walking tours, panoramic bus circuits, and viewpoint routes via the Yanahuara and Carmen Alto miradors. The three-hour city walk covers the main plaza, Santa Catalina Monastery and the volcanic sillar streetscape. They also run combined day trips that include both an Arequipa city tour and a Colca Canyon excursion — useful for travellers on a tight schedule who want to cover both in one day.
