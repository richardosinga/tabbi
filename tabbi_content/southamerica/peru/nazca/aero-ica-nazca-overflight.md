Aero Ica is one of the oldest aviation companies in Peru's south coast and offers the most affordable flights from a licensed, safety-certified operator. The 30-minute Cessna overflight covers the main geoglyphs; because Cessna 172/182 aircraft bank steeply to show figures to both sides, all passengers get clear views. Passengers are weighed before boarding and the pilot adjusts seating accordingly. Office is in the Nazca city centre; airport transfers are included. Book 24 hours ahead in peak season.
