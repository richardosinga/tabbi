AeroPanam has operated overflights from Maria Reiche Airport for over two decades and maintains a strong safety record. Their Cessna Caravan seats up to eight passengers and is wider than the smaller Cessnas used by some competitors, reducing the sensation of turbulence. The 35-minute flight covers twelve figures including the Hummingbird, Spider, Monkey, Condor and Astronaut. The co-pilot provides live commentary in English and Spanish. Booking in advance is strongly recommended from June through October; walk-up flights are available in quieter months.
