Lima Tours is one of Peru's largest and most reputable agencies, and their Nazca day trip is the premium option for travellers who don't want to overnight in Nazca. A scheduled flight from Lima Jorge Chávez Airport takes 45 minutes to Ica/Nazca; the overflight happens before lunch, followed by a guided visit to the Chauchilla open-air cemetery (with mummies visible in situ) and the Cahuachi ceremonial complex. Return flight is in the afternoon. The all-in pricing covers transfers, flights and guiding but is substantially more expensive than organising the overflight independently from Nazca.
