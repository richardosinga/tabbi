The Nazca Lines are best understood from the air. The geoglyphs — hummingbirds, spider, astronaut, hands, monkey and dozens of geometric lines — are too large to comprehend from ground level; the observation tower only reveals one or two trapezoids. Small-plane overflights typically last 30–35 minutes and cover around a dozen of the main figures. Flights depart from Maria Reiche Neumann Airport just outside Nazca city, with multiple daily departures in clear conditions. The experience is turbulent and not suitable for those with motion sickness. Always compare safety records before booking.
