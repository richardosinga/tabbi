Andean Kingdom is one of Huaraz's better-regarded adventure agencies, with certified mountain guides (UIAGM) and strong logistics for altitude trekking. Their Huascarán day hike ascends into the Huascarán National Park to the moraine at around 4,800m, where the hanging glaciers of the highest peak in Peru loom above. The terrain is more technical than Laguna 69 and crampons may be required; the guide assesses conditions on the day. Good fitness and a minimum of three nights' acclimatisation are required. An extraordinary experience for those prepared for it.
