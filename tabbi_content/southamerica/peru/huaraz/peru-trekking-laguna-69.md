Laguna 69 is Huaraz's signature day hike and this is the standard guided version. The lake sits at 4,604m in the Llanganuco valley beneath the sheer north face of Chopicalqui. The trail gains 900m in 7 km from the trailhead; the round trip takes 5–6 hours of hiking. The guide keeps the group together on a sustainable pace — critical at altitude. Transport from Huaraz, park entrance fee and lunch are included. Recommend two nights' acclimatisation in Huaraz (3,052m) before attempting this hike. Not suitable for people with heart or respiratory conditions.
