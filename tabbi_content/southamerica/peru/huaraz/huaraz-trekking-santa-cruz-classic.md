Huaraz Trekking is a locally owned agency that has been running Santa Cruz departures since the early 2000s. Their classic package covers the same four-day route as competitors at a slightly lower price by using local food suppliers and community-based campsites. The guide (Spanish and English) is UIAGM-certified. Daily distances are 12–14 km on defined trails; the highest point is Punta Unión at 4,750m. Rental gear is available for hire if you don't have your own sleeping bag rated to -10°C. A departure-guaranteed option allows solo travellers to join group departures.
