The Cordillera Blanca north of Huaraz is the highest tropical mountain range on earth, with 27 peaks above 6,000 metres and more glaciated summits than anywhere outside the Himalayas and Andes proper. The classic day hike to **Laguna 69** (4,600m) is one of Peru's most photographed destinations. The **Santa Cruz Trek** (four days, up to 4,750m) and the **Cordillera Huayhuash Circuit** (eight to twelve days) are among the world's finest multi-day treks. Acclimatisation in Huaraz for at least two days before any high-altitude hike is essential; altitude sickness is a real risk above 4,000m.
