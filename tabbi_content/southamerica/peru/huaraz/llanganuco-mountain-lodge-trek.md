The Llanganuco Mountain Lodge sits at 3,800m in the valley between Huascarán and Chopicalqui and offers a comfortable alternative to camping for trekkers who want altitude without sleeping under canvas. Their three-day guided trek uses the lodge as a base for day hikes up to the Llanganuco lakes and the Huascarán base camp approach, returning to the lodge for dinner and a proper bed each night. The food is excellent for this altitude. A good option for older trekkers, families or anyone wanting to experience the Cordillera Blanca without full camping logistics.
