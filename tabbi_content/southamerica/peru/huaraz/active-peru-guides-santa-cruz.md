The Santa Cruz Trek is a loop through the core of the Cordillera Blanca, passing beneath Taulliraju (5,830m) and through the Punta Unión pass at 4,750m. Active Peru Guides runs this as a fully catered expedition with a certified guide, a camp cook and mule support for equipment. The four days cover 50 km and include three nights under the stars at altitude. The included meals, equipment and mule support make this more expensive than basic operators but substantially more comfortable. A good choice for trekkers who haven't done multi-day high-altitude camping before.
