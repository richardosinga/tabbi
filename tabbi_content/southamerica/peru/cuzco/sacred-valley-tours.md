The Sacred Valley of the Incas stretches northwest of Cuzco along the Urubamba River, and a day tour here is often the best introduction to Inca culture before tackling Machu Picchu. Standard routes stop at the Pisac ruins and Sunday market, the massive hilltop fortress of Ollantaytambo, and the salt pans of Maras. More flexible operators let you combine the valley with an afternoon train connection to Aguas Calientes. Most tours depart Cuzco by 8am and return by 7pm.
