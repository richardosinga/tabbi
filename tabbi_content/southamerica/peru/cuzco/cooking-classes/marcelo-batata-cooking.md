Marcelo Batata runs a cooking school out of a colonial house in Cusco, offering a more intimate alternative to the larger market-to-kitchen class format. The 2.5-hour session concentrates on highland Peruvian dishes, using Andean ingredients to prepare a small menu of traditional recipes. The colonial setting adds atmosphere, and the small group size means participants get direct attention from the instructor throughout. A solid option if you'd prefer to skip the market walk and go straight into cooking.
