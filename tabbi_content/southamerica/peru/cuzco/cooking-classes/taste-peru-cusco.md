Taste Peru's Cusco class begins with a guided walk through the San Pedro market to select the day's ingredients, then moves into a kitchen session focused squarely on highland Andean cooking. The menu centres on quinoa soup, rocoto relleno (Cusco's signature stuffed pepper dish) and picarones (Peruvian doughnuts). Groups are capped at eight, keeping the class hands-on throughout. A good choice for anyone wanting to cook dishes that are genuinely Andean rather than coastal Peruvian cuisine.
