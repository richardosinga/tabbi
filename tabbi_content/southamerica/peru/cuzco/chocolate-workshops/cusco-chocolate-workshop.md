This family-run workshop in the San Blas neighbourhood is one of the most affordable chocolate-making experiences in Cusco. The 1.5-hour session walks participants through the full bean-to-bar process: roasting, grinding and tempering cacao sourced from Peruvian farms. Each guest leaves with the chocolate bar they made. The setting is intimate and the pace unhurried, making it a good option for families or anyone wanting a low-key introduction to Peruvian cacao.
