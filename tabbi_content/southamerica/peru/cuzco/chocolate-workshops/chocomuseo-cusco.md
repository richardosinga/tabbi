ChocoMuseo is a well-established Peruvian chocolate education brand with a dedicated Cusco workshop and retail shop. The bean-to-bar session covers roasting, grinding and moulding using Amazonian cacao, and participants leave with the bars they made. The operation is more polished than some local alternatives, with clear instruction and good infrastructure for groups including children. Their retail shop sells drinking chocolate, cacao nibs and finished bars to take home.
