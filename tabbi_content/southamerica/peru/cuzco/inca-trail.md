The Classic Inca Trail is one of the world's great long-distance walks: four days from the Sacred Valley through high-altitude passes, Inca ruins and cloud forest to the Sun Gate above Machu Picchu. The government caps permits at 500 per day (including guides and porters), so availability is limited and popular departure dates fill up six months or more ahead. All treks must be booked through a licensed operator who handles permits, guides and cook staff. The trail reaches 4,215 m at Dead Woman's Pass — acclimatising in Cusco for at least two days beforehand is essential.
