ChocoMuseo is a small chain with locations across Peru and Central America, and the Cuzco branch is one of the best. The two-hour workshop walks participants through every stage of chocolate-making using Amazonian cacao, and you leave with the bars you made. The space is comfortable and the instructors are patient with groups that include children. Walk-ins are usually possible midweek; advance booking is recommended at weekends. Their shop downstairs sells drinking chocolate and cacao nibs to take home.
