Viajes Pacifico is one of the more reliable local Cuzco operators for Sacred Valley day trips, keeping groups under 12 and using guides who are Cuzco-born with strong knowledge of local archaeology. The standard tour covers Pisac ruins (above the market, not just the market), Ollantaytambo and the Chinchero weaving cooperative. Lunch is included at a local restaurant in the valley. The price is among the most competitive for the quality of guiding.
