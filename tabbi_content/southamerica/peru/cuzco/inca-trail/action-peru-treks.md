Action Peru Treks is an award-winning Cusco operator with a well-established reputation for ethical labour practices, including fair wages and proper equipment for porters — an important consideration on a regulated trail where porter welfare standards vary significantly between operators. Inca Trail permits are included in the price and are handled entirely by the agency. Groups are capped at 16, guides are certified, and the four-day itinerary follows the classic route finishing at the Sun Gate above Machu Picchu.
