Siete Angelitos operates a compact cooking school in the bohemian San Blas quarter of Cuzco, a ten-minute uphill walk from the Plaza de Armas. The class is more relaxed in style than some competitors — a maximum of ten guests, a conversational pace and an emphasis on everyday Andean cooking rather than restaurant techniques. You'll prepare *sopa de quinoa*, *rocoto relleno* and a native-potato dish, then eat with the instructor. A morning market walk at San Pedro can be added for an extra fee.
