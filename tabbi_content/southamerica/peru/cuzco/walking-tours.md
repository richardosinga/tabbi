Cusco rewards slow exploration on foot. The historic centre is compact enough to cover in half a day, but the layers of Inca stonework beneath colonial facades, the steep alleys of San Blas and the base walls of Sacsayhuamán are easy to miss without a guide who knows what to look for. Most walking tours focus on the Plaza de Armas and the Qorikancha sun temple, with better operators extending into the San Blas barrio and the carved Inca laneways nearby. Tours typically run 2.5–3 hours and are best done on arrival to orient yourself before venturing further afield.
