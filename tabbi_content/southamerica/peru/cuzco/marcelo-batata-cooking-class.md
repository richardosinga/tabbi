Marcelo Batata is a Cuzco institution: chef, restaurateur and cooking teacher whose eponymous restaurant on Calle Palacio is one of the best in the city. His cooking classes are limited to eight participants and begin with an accompanied market visit to San Pedro, where he explains Andean ingredients — *ají amarillo*, *huacatay*, native potatoes and *kiwicha* — before heading to his kitchen. The class covers four courses, participants eat the results, and it finishes with pisco cocktails. The most personalised option in Cuzco.
