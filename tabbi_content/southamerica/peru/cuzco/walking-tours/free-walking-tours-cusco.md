Free Walking Tours Cusco runs daily English-language tours of the historic centre on a tip-based model. Guides are local university students with training in archaeology, which means the commentary on Inca stonework and the Qorikancha sun temple is more substantive than many paid alternatives. The 2.5-hour route covers Plaza de Armas, the colonial streets overlying Inca foundations, San Blas and the lower terraces of Sacsayhuamán. No advance booking required; tours depart from the Plaza de Armas.
