Cusco Walks offers small-group walking tours led by licensed archaeologist guides, making this a good choice for anyone who wants to go beyond the standard highlights. Tours can be customised to focus on Inca stonemason techniques — the fitted polygonal walls, the trapezoidal doorways — and can include less-visited laneways and courtyards that most tourists walk past. The standard three-hour tour covers the historic centre and San Blas; private departures can be arranged at any time.
