SAM Travel is a family-run Cusco agency with a community tourism focus, meaning a portion of tour proceeds goes directly to the villages visited along the route. Their Sacred Valley day tour covers the Pisac market, the hilltop fortress of Ollantaytambo and the weaving demonstrations at Chinchero. Groups are kept small, guides are bilingual and locally trained, and the operator is well regarded for genuine engagement with Quechua communities rather than a surface-level visit.
