Kondor Path is a locally owned Cusco agency whose Sacred Valley day tours are led by certified bilingual guides born and raised in the region. The itinerary follows the standard circuit of Pisac ruins, Ollantaytambo and Chinchero, but includes a sit-down lunch stop at a restaurant in Urubamba town — a more authentic midday break than the buffet lunch options used by larger operators. At $40 per person it is one of the better-value options for the quality of guiding on offer.
