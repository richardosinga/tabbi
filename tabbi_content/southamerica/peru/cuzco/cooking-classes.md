Peruvian cooking classes in Cuzco typically begin with a guided walk through the San Pedro market to select ingredients, then move into a well-equipped kitchen to prepare three or four dishes. *Causa limeña*, *lomo saltado*, quinoa soups and Andean stews feature regularly. Most classes end with eating what you cooked, with pisco sours. The altitude can affect both cooking times and appetites — instructors adjust accordingly. Classes run for three to four hours and are suitable for all skill levels.
