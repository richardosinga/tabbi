G Adventures' Sacred Valley and Machu Picchu combination is one of the best-value ways to see the full Inca heartland without organising everything independently. The three-day trip departs Cuzco, overnights in the Sacred Valley, continues to Aguas Calientes by train, and visits Machu Picchu the following morning before returning to Cuzco by afternoon. Group size is capped at twelve. Train tickets and hotel nights are included; park entrance to Machu Picchu is an additional cost booked separately.
