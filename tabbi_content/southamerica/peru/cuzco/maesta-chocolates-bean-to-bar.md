Maesta is a Cuzco-based artisan chocolatier whose workshop is a step up from the standard tourist experience. The 2.5-hour session covers the theory of cacao origin and flavour before moving into hands-on production. Participants taste cacao from multiple Peruvian regions side by side before making their own bars. The maximum group size is eight, making this a more intimate experience. It requires advance booking and is not suitable for children under ten. Located in the San Blas neighbourhood.
