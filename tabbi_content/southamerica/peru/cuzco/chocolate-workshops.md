Peru produces some of the finest cacao in the world, and Cuzco's chocolate workshops offer a chance to understand how it goes from tropical pod to finished bar. Most workshops last 1.5–2 hours and cover roasting, winnowing, grinding and tempering, ending with tasting or moulding your own chocolate. The best operators source single-origin cacao from Amazonian farms and explain the flavour differences between regions. A good rainy-day activity and a popular option for families.
