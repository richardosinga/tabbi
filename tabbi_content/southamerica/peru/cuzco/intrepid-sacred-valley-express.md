Intrepid's one-day Sacred Valley trip is aimed at independent travellers who want a structured guided experience without committing to a multi-day tour. The itinerary prioritises Pisac ruins in the morning (before crowds arrive), allows two hours at the Sunday market, then moves on to Ollantaytambo in the afternoon. A local guide leads the group throughout. Pickup from central Cuzco hotels is included. This is a good option if you've already arranged Machu Picchu separately and want to fill the days before or after.
