Titicaca Travel Peru runs the standard Puno–Copacabana–La Paz tourist route as a full-day transfer. The journey begins with hotel pickup in Puno, crosses the Peruvian border at Yunguyo, stops in Copacabana on the Bolivian shore, and includes the catamaran crossing of the Tiquina strait where passengers and the bus cross separately — a brief but characteristic moment on the lake. A local lunch stop is included, and the same operator handles logistics on both the Peruvian and Bolivian sides. Arrival in La Paz is typically late afternoon. A straightforward, well-organised border run on one of South America's most travelled tourist corridors.
