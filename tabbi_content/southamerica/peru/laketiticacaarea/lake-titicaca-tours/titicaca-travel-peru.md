Titicaca Travel Peru is a Puno-based operator with direct ownership ties to the lake communities. Their standard full-day tour covers Uros and Taquile; an overnight extension adds a night with a Taquile family, dinner prepared in the home, and an early-morning walk on the island before the day-trip boats arrive. The guides speak Quechua as well as Spanish and English, which makes a difference on Taquile — the island's weaving cooperative and textile traditions are explained from the inside rather than as a tourist presentation. A solid, community-connected operator at a fair price.
