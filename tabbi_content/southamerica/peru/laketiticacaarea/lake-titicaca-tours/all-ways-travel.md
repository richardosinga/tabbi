All Ways Travel was founded by Gloria Villanueva, one of the first woman-owned travel agencies operating on Lake Titicaca, and the agency has maintained a strong community tourism model since its founding. A direct share of the income from island stays goes to the islander families rather than through intermediaries, and guides maintain long-standing relationships with the communities on Uros, Taquile and Amantaní. The full tour range covers a single full day on Uros and Taquile up to a three-day circuit adding Amantaní overnight and the Sillustani pre-Inca chullpa tombs outside Puno. Also offers Puno city tours. One of the most ethically grounded operators on the lake.
