G Adventures' Lake Titicaca Express adds a third island, Amantaní, to the standard Uros-Taquile circuit and includes a night with an Amantaní family (one of the island's genuine homestay traditions). Day one covers Uros and arrives at Amantaní for overnight. Day two is Taquile. Day three returns to Puno. This is the recommended option for travellers who want a full Lake Titicaca experience rather than a single-day overview. Group size is capped at twelve; the itinerary runs as a small-group guided tour with full logistics included.
