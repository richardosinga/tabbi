The overland route from Puno in Peru to La Paz in Bolivia is one of the classic South American border crossings, and the tourist bus services that run it make the logistics straightforward. The route passes through Copacabana on the Bolivian shore of Lake Titicaca, and includes a short catamaran crossing of the Tiquina strait where the bus and passengers cross separately by boat. Total journey time is roughly seven to eight hours including the border stop. Organised transfers handle hotel pickup, border paperwork assistance and a lunch stop; independent travel is possible but the organised route is better value and significantly less stressful for the border process.
