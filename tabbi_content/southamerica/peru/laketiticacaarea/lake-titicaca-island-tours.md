Lake Titicaca sits at 3,812 metres above sea level and covers an area the size of Puerto Rico. The two main tour destinations from Puno are the Uros floating islands — a pre-Inca civilisation living on artificial reed platforms, now partly tourist-facing — and Taquile Island, a natural island with Inca terracing and a living weaving tradition recognised by UNESCO. Day tours cover both; overnight stays on Taquile or with Uros families offer a more authentic experience. The lake is cold and exposed — warm layers are essential regardless of season.
