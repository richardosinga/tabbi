SAS Travel (Cuzco-based but operating from Puno) runs a reliable full-day Lake Titicaca tour that sits between the basic local operators and the premium agencies on price and quality. The English guide is knowledgeable about Titicaca ecology and Uros culture, and lunch on Taquile — trout, quinoa soup, purple potato — is included in the price. Groups average ten to fourteen people. They depart from their Puno office; hotel pickup can be arranged. A solid mid-range choice for travellers passing through Puno on the Lima–Cuzco–Bolivia circuit.
