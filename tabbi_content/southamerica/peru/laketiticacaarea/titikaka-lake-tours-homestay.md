Overnight on Taquile transforms the experience from a tourist sight to something more genuine. This two-day package covers the Uros islands on day one, then proceeds to Taquile where guests sleep in a family home (basic but clean), eat dinner prepared by the host family, and wake for sunrise over the lake. Morning on the island before the day-trippers arrive from Puno is entirely different: the stone terraces are quiet, the weavers are at work, and the lack of boats makes the altitude feel complete. Return to Puno by lunchtime on day two.
