All Ways Travel is a Puno-based cooperative that has worked directly with the Uros and Taquile communities for over 25 years. This matters: a larger portion of the tour price reaches the communities, and the guides are often from the islands themselves. The full-day tour leaves Puno port at 7:30am, reaches the Uros islands by 8:30am for a 1-hour community visit (including reed boat ride), then sails three hours to Taquile for lunch and an afternoon on the terraces. Back in Puno by 5pm. One of the most ethical operators on the lake.
