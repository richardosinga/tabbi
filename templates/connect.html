Use Tabbi in Claude

Connect Tabbi to Claude as an MCP integration and plan trips directly from any conversation.

What you can do

  • plan_trip Create a new multi-city trip plan
  • open_plan Open an existing trip using your passphrase
  • research_city Look up things to do in a city
  • add_pois_to_plan Add places directly to a stop on your trip
  • submit_pois Submit newly researched places to your plan
  • search_world66 Search the world66 travel guide

How to connect

  1. Open Claude settings → Integrations
  2. Click Add integration and paste this URL:
    https://tab.bi/mcp
  3. Save and start a new conversation. Tell Claude your trip — it will use Tabbi automatically.
Open Claude integrations →
Your trips are protected by a passphrase. Claude will ask for it the first time you open a plan, and remembers it for the rest of the conversation.

Don't have Claude?

You need a Claude.ai account (free or Pro). MCP integrations are available on the web app.
