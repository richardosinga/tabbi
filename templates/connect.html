Use Tabbi in Claude

Connect Tabbi as an MCP integration and plan trips directly from any Claude conversation.

1

Open Claude integrations

Go to claude.ai → Settings → Integrations and click Add integration.

2

Paste the Tabbi URL

https://tab.bi/mcp
3

Start planning

Open a new Claude conversation and tell it where you want to go. Claude will ask a few questions and build your plan.

Open Claude integrations → How it works

What Claude can do with Tabbi

plan_trip Create a new multi-city trip plan
open_plan Open an existing trip using your passphrase
research_city Find the best places to visit, eat, and drink in a city
add_pois_to_plan Add world66 places directly to a stop
submit_pois Submit newly researched places to your plan
remove_poi_from_plan Remove a place from a stop
search_world66 Search the world66 travel guide
geocode Look up coordinates for a place (used for map pins)
No account needed. Your trips are protected by a passphrase. Claude will ask for it the first time you open a plan, and remembers it for the rest of the conversation.
