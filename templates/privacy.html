Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Tabbi is a trip-planning tool. This policy covers the Tabbi website (tab.bi) and the Tabbi Trip Planner browser extension.

What we collect

When you create a trip plan, we store the plan title, city stops, and any places you add. Plans are identified by a passphrase you choose — we do not require an account or email address.

The browser extension stores your server URL and trip passphrases locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local. This data never leaves your browser except when making requests to the Tabbi server you have configured.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, or any identifying personal information. We do not use advertising trackers or sell data to third parties.

Page content sent to Tabbi

When you use the browser extension to add a place to a trip, the extension may send the URL and a text extract of the current page to the Tabbi server so that an AI model can identify the relevant place. This content is used only to process your request and is not stored beyond that.

AI processing

Tabbi uses the Anthropic Claude API to help identify and describe places. Page content sent for this purpose is subject to Anthropic's privacy policy.

Cookies and analytics

Tabbi uses session cookies to keep you signed in. We do not run third-party analytics.
