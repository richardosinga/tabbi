Plan trips with AI, keep them forever

Connect Tabbi to your AI assistant and plan your next trip in a conversation. It asks what you want, finds the best places, and builds a shareable plan — with a map, budget tracker, and curated stops for every city.

Connect your AI → Start manually
1

Connect Tabbi to your AI

Add https://tab.bi/mcp as an integration in Claude, Cursor, Windsurf, or any MCP-compatible assistant. Takes 30 seconds — no account needed. Or install the browser extension to add places while you browse.

See all connection options →
2

Tell it your trip

Just describe what you want — "10 days in Japan in October, two adults, into food and temples, relaxed pace". Your AI asks a few questions, then gets to work.

3

Get your plan

Your AI creates a plan with a stop for each city, curated places to visit, eat, and drink — drawn from the world66 travel guide and its own research. Each place has a description, map pin, and category.

4

Share and refine

Your plan is yours forever. Share the link with anyone. Add or remove places. Track your budget. Come back to your AI anytime to change things up.

What's in a Tabbi plan

🗺️

Interactive map

Every place pinned on a map per stop, with dark/light mode toggle.

🎯

Do / Eat / Drink

Places grouped by type so you can see your day at a glance.

💰

Budget tracker

Add your daily budget per stop and track totals across the whole trip.

🔑

Passphrase access

No login required. Your plan is protected by a passphrase you can share with travel companions.

📖

World66 content

Places link to the world66 travel guide for deeper reading on each destination.

✏️

Fully editable

Add notes, remove places, edit the plan directly — or ask your AI to do it for you.
