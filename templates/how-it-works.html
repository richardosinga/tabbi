Connect Tabbi to Claude

How to connect →
2

Tell Claude your trip

Just describe what you want — "10 days in Japan in October, two adults, into food and temples, relaxed pace". Claude asks a few questions, then gets to work.

3

Get your plan

Claude creates a plan with a stop for each city, curated places to visit, eat, and drink — drawn from the world66 travel guide and its own research. Each place has a description, map pin, and category.

4

Share and refine

Your plan is yours forever. Share the link with anyone. Add or remove places. Track your budget. Come back to Claude anytime to change things up.

What's in a Tabbi plan

🗺️

Interactive map

Every place pinned on a map per stop, with dark/light mode toggle.

🎯

Do / Eat / Drink

Places grouped by type so you can see your day at a glance.

💰

Budget tracker

Add your daily budget per stop and track totals across the whole trip.

🔑

Passphrase access

No login required. Your plan is protected by a passphrase you can share with travel companions.

📖

World66 content

Places link to the world66 travel guide for deeper reading on each destination.

✏️

Fully editable

Add notes, remove places, edit the plan directly — or ask Claude to do it for you.
